# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses and instructors sell them. This glossary fixes the words we use in the product, in emails and in code.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A member of our staff who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Moderator, using "admin" for a Team Admin

**Team Admin**:
A team member who manages a Team Licence and its Seats. A Team Admin is not an Admin.
_Avoid_: Admin, team owner

### Buying a course

**Purchase**:
One payment by a user for one course, either for themselves or as a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course. It ends when a Refund is made for the Purchase that gave it.
_Avoid_: Subscription, access grant

### Teams

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase of a course for a team, split into Seats that members claim.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, team purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence, which gives one member an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Coupon, voucher

**Seat Code**:
The code a team member enters to claim a Seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon code, redemption code

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a team member has already taken with its Seat Code.
_Avoid_: Redeemed coupon, used seat

### Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A request, in the purchaser's own words, for us to return the money for one whole Purchase. Only the person who made the Purchase can make one, and a Purchase has at most one. It is Requested, then an Admin Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Cancellation, Chargeback, Pending Chargeback, Cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
Money we actually return to the learner. It exists only once a Refund Request has been Approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Payback

**Refund Amount**:
The amount a learner gets back: exactly what they paid for that Purchase, even if that differs from the course's current list price.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, including all of the fourteenth day, during which a Refund Request can be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can have a Refund Request made: it must be within the Refund Window, with under two hours of video watched, fewer than half the course's lessons completed, and no Claimed Seats. It is judged when the Refund Request is made, not when it is decided.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refundable

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund Request we have received and not yet decided.
_Avoid_: Pending, Raised, Open

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request an Admin has accepted. It results in a Refund and ends the Enrollment.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request an Admin has turned down. No money is returned, and the decision is final.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Chargeback**:
A dispute a cardholder raises through their bank to claw back a payment, without asking us. It is not a Refund Request.
